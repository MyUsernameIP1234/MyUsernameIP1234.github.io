        <h2  id = "Header2"   class="italic" >Recipe #1: Creamy Garlic Prawn Pasta</h2>
        
        <ul>
            <li>Time: 25 minutes</li>
            <li>Serves: 4</li>
        </ul>
        <img src="https://cdn.kastatic.org/third_party/javascript-khansrc/live-editor/build/images/food/pasta.png" width="350" >
        <table>
            <thead>
                <tr>
                    <th><br>Ingredients</th>
                    <th><br>Quantity</th>
                </tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
                <tr><td>Pasta noodles</td><td>| 350 grams</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Thickened cream</td><td>| 1 1/4 cups</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Peeled prawns</td><td>| 500 grams</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Unsalted butter</td><td>| 50 grams</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Chicken broth</td><td>| 1/2 cup</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Garlic</td><td>| 4 cloves, finely minced</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Dry white wine</td><td>| 1/2 cup</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Parmesan cheese</td><td>| 3/4 cup (75g), finely grated</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Fresh parsley</td><td>| 2 tbsp, finely chopped (optional)</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Black pepper</td><td>| to taste</td></tr>
            </tbody>
        </table>
        
        <p><strong>Step 1:</strong> Cook pasta. Bring to a boil in a large pot of water. Add a large pinch of salt and cook for however long the pasta packet says minus 1 minute.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 2:</strong> Once your pasta is cooked, throw out the water minus one cup. We will use it to thicken the sauce.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 3:</strong> While the pasta cooks, melt about a third of the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook half the prawns for 1.5 minutes, flip, and cook for another 1–1.5 minutes until barely done. Remove the prawns to a bowl and repeat with another third of the butter and the remaining prawns.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 4:</strong> In the same skillet, melt the remaining butter. Add the garlic and stir for 20 seconds until fragrant—do not let it burn.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 5:</strong> Pour in the wine and simmer rapidly for 2 minutes, scraping the pan to lift any browned bits, until the wine mostly evaporates.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 6:</strong> Add the cream, chicken broth, and parmesan. Stir until the cheese melts, then simmer for 1.5 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 7:</strong> Return the prawns to the pan along with the drained pasta and about 1/4 cup of the reserved pasta water. Toss over medium-high heat for 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens and clings to the pasta. If it gets too thick, add a little more reserved pasta water.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 8:</strong> Stir through most of the parsley and a grind of black pepper. Taste and add salt only if needed. Serve immediately, garnished with the remaining parsley and extra parmesan if you like.</p>
        
        <p><em>Source: <a href="https://www.recipetineats.com/creamy-garlic-prawn-pasta/">RecipeTin Eats – Creamy Garlic Prawn Pasta</a></em></p>

        <h2 id="Header3" class = "italic"  >Recipe #2: Butter Chicken</h2>
        
        <ul>
            <li>Time: 35 minutes (plus 3+ hours marinating)</li>
            <li>Serves: 3–4</li>
        </ul>
        <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/41/Butter_Chicken_%26_Butter_Naan_-_Home_-_Chandigarh_-_India_-_0006.jpg/960px-Butter_Chicken_%26_Butter_Naan_-_Home_-_Chandigarh_-_India_-_0006.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=imageinfo&utm_content=thumbnail" width="300" >
        <table>
            <thead>
                <tr>
                    <th><br>Ingredients</th>
                    <th><br>Quantity</th>
                </tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
                <tr><td>Plain yogurt (full fat)</td><td>| 1/2 cup</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Lemon juice</td><td>| 1 tbsp</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Turmeric powder</td><td>| 1 tsp</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Garam masala</td><td>| 2 tsp</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Chilli powder or cayenne pepper</td><td>| 1/2 tsp</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Ground cumin</td><td>| 1 tsp</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Fresh ginger, grated</td><td>| 1 tbsp</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Garlic, crushed</td><td>| 2 cloves</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Chicken thigh fillets, bite-size pieces</td><td>| 750 g (1.5 lb)</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Butter</td><td>| 2 tbsp (30 g)</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Tomato passata (tomato puree)</td><td>| 1 cup</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Heavy / thickened cream</td><td>| 1 cup</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Sugar</td><td>| 1 tbsp</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Salt</td><td>| 1 1/4 tsp</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Basmati rice (to serve)</td><td>| as needed</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Coriander/cilantro (optional garnish)</td><td>| to taste</td></tr>
            </tbody>
        </table>
        
        <p><strong>Step 1:</strong> Marinade: Combine all the Marinade ingredients (yogurt, lemon juice, turmeric, garam masala, chilli powder, cumin, ginger, garlic) with the chicken in a bowl. Cover and refrigerate overnight, or for at least 3 hours.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 2:</strong> Cook chicken: Heat the butter over high heat in a large fry pan. Take the chicken out of the marinade (do not shake off the marinade) and place it in the pan. Cook for around 3 minutes, or until the chicken is white all over. Do not pour the leftover marinade from the bowl into the pan yet.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 3:</strong> Sauce: Add the tomato passata, cream, sugar, and salt to the pan. Also add any remaining marinade left in the bowl. Turn down to low and simmer for 20 minutes. Taste and add more salt if needed.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 4:</strong> Garnish with coriander/cilantro leaves if using. Serve with basmati rice.</p>
        
        <p><em>Source: <a href="https://www.recipetineats.com/butter-chicken/">RecipeTin Eats – Butter Chicken</a></em></p>

        <p><strong>Step 1:</strong> Take your frozen mango chunks and put them all int the blender. Add the water and the sugar and turn it on.</p>

        <p><strong>Step 2:</strong> Once it's no longer really blending and the mango is stuck to the sites, turn off the blender and scrape down the sides with a spoon. Turn the blender bacsk on and repeat until you have a frozen mango puree.</p>

          <p><strong>Step 3:</strong> Serve and enjoy.</p>
